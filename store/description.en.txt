Party Challenges turns your Autodarts board into a challenge card party game, right inside play.autodarts.com.

How it works
• Each turn, the player picks a level: Easy (1 point), Medium (2 points) or Hard (3 points).
• They draw a challenge card such as "Hit the 20 twice" or "Check out 40" and throw their turn.
• Solve the card and you get its points. Fail, and the next player tries the same card until someone solves it.
• The first player to reach the target score wins (10 points by default).

Your Autodarts board detects every dart, so the cards are checked automatically, including corrections and undo.

Getting started
Open Play → Party → Party Challenges, add the players, select your board and start the game.

• 60 challenge cards in three levels
• English and German (follows your browser, adjustable in the settings)
• Adjustable target score
• Collects no data and sends nothing anywhere
