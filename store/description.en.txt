Party Challenges turns your Autodarts board into a challenge card party game, right inside play.autodarts.com.

Each turn, the player picks a level (Easy, Medium or Hard), draws a challenge card such as "Hit the 20 twice" or "Check out 40" and throws their turn. Your Autodarts board detects every dart, so the cards are checked automatically, including corrections and undo.

Two ways to play

Classic
• A solved card is worth 1, 2 or 3 points, depending on its level.
• Fail, and the next player tries the same card until someone solves it.
• The first player to reach the target score wins (10 points by default).

Betting
• Everyone starts with 100 coins. You see the card first, then bet on it.
• A solved card pays your bet times its level, plus the pot of all failed bets.
• Extras like doubters, calling your shot and golden cards add twists. Most coins wins.

Getting started
Open Play → Party → Party Challenges, add the players, select your board, choose Classic or Betting and start the game.

• 60 challenge cards in three levels
• English and German (follows your browser, adjustable in the settings)
• Adjustable target score and betting rules
• Collects no data and sends nothing anywhere
